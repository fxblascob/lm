"Towards New Organisational patterns for the Management of Information Resources at the University Campus"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EUNIS'97: European Cooperation in Higher Education Information Systems. Grenoble, France. (Septiembre, 1997).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dr. Enrique Rubio Royo (rubio@cicei.ulpgc.es)
Dr. Antonio Ocon Carreras (ocon@cicei.ulpgc.es)
Dr. Luis Alvarez Alvarez (alvarez@cicei.ulpgc.es)

CICEI - University of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Abstract

We are now living a process of generalised change that leads towards the society of information or knowledge. It is necessary for any organisation to undertake the management of this cultural and technological change as a means to adapt to this evolution.

At the level of the "Campus", the adjustment of higher education institutions imply two main challenges:

  1. new teaching and investigation paradigms
  2. new organisational patterns for the management of the information resources.

The Infomation Tecnology paradiarising during the 90s have caused not only an evolution of the data processing methods but also a new model of computer science for the end-product consumer. This model is based on:

  1. the strategic value of co-operation and collaboration
  2. the promotion of the end-product consumer; and
  3. a new role of the computing services ruled by two principles: to make easy and co-ordinate.

In this sense, users are more concerned about finding and accessing, at a local level, the information resources and services are organised or where they are phisically placed. Thus, This fact should lead their management.

Therefore, the aim of the "Information Resource Management" shold be to plan and to provide high quality information resources and services, as support for teachers and researchers in the whole Campus, as well as optimising the university's administrative resources.

The University of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria (ULPGC), starting from the experience of the Computing and Communications Center of the Engineering Building (CICEI); is immersed in a process of institutional training, as a means to adapt to the above mentioned challenges.

In this sense, and related to the design of a new organisational model, a new Information Technology Co-ordinator (CIO) has been appointed. Besides, the CICEI is now a General Service of R&D of the ULPGC. Its main objective is to "set up and divulge a new campus model, based on the introduction and integration of I.T.s, which would facilitate (and not with unendurable costs) teaching and research at the university, as well as the services associated to it".

At the same time, we try to dicide the management of the Information Resources into four main groups:

  1. Academic/Institutional Support Services
  2. Administrative Computing Services
  3. Institutional Investigation Office
  4. Telecommunication Services

Each one has different functional units. Because of this, there are two separate commissions for the information technology, with technical and institutional characteristics.
