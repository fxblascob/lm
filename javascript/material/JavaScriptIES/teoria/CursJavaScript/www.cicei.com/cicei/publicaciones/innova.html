"INNOVA Project: A proposal for fostering electronic teaching and learning based on the WWW"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EUNIS'97: European Cooperation in Higher Education Information Systems.
Grenoble, France. (Septiembre, 1997).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dr. Enrique Rubio Royo (rubio@cicei.com)
Dr. Antonio Ocón Carreras (ocon@cicei.com)
Dr. Manuel J. Galán Moreno (manolo@cicei.com)

CICEI - University of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Abstract

As a development of the RUBICON Innovation Campus Model, and covered by the Information Technologies Plan of the University of Las Palmas, on the scope of an "Plan de Mejora de la Calidad de la Docencia" (Project for Improvement of Teaching Skills), the INNOVA PROJECT recently was presented.

As the title suggests, the INNOVA Project is a proposal to promote teaching and learning by electronic media, sustantially based on the WWW, in as much to use and exploit the investment of the university in its corporate ATM networking (ULPnet). Specifically, the project addresses the "On Line Learning and Teaching inside an Information-Rich Environment", with especial emphasis in the following applications:

  1. Shared and widespread scientific and technical matters.
  2. An specific curricula (Technical School of Industrial Engineering)
  3. Wide-use standard software applications (WWW software and AutoCAD)

All this supported by several other Information Services and resources:

  1. "Aula de Idiomas de la ULPGC" (Language Facilities of the ULPGC)
  2. "Aula de Informática de Ciencias del Mar" (Computer Facilities of the Sea Sciences School)
  3. "Biblioteca del Edificio de Ingenierias" (Engineering Library)
  4. "Servicio de Reprografía" (Publications Services)
  5. CEANI (Numeric Applications in Engineering Center)
  6. CICEI (Computing and Communications Center of Engineering Building)

Detailded seeked in this Innovation Project, different from the traditional approach is :"Local and remote learning, based on WWW technologies and Methodologies, Internet and Intranet" (electronic learning environment).

The new trends on training and learning, require an urgent innovation, based on the Information Technologies, which carries along the need to develop Information/Training Networks, with a different overview of the Time and Space Concepts applied to Knowledge Transmission. All of this implied a "brand new" kind of trainer and trainee mentality, well suited to this Technologies. Hence the need to promote a new concept of "Meta-Training" emphasizing the use of all this new available resources, which appears to be one of the most urgently fields of interest.

The teachers and matters involved in this project are 30, while the total number of students involved is 4176.
