"Metabolization of Information in Emerging Organizational Structures: The SITIO-ULPGC environment".
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Proceedings of the Second Conference on Projectics, (Bayona, Francia; Octubre, 1996).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dr. Enrique Rubio Royo (rubio@cicei.ulpgc.es)
Dr. Antonio Ocon Carreras (ocon@cicei.ulpgc.es)
Pablo Vazquez Ramirez (pablo@cicei.ulpgc.es)

CICEI - University of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Keywords

Information Technologies, Information Management, Organizational Change, Process Reingenieering, Process Infrastructures, Computing Sciences.

Abstract

In the transition from an Industrial Economy towards an Information Economy, it is required to design new organization structures to support and keep up the new competitive strategies in corporations. This new view, dictated by a new global environment, considers the organization as a "Learning Organism" which is based on a corporative network of shared information. The following criteria are assumed:

  • (a) Information is a fundamental resource such as raw materials, being the fundamental nutrient to be transformed into knowledge and know-how (ground of every competitive strategy);
  • (b) Any member of the organization is an information consumer, processor and generator. From such a perspective, it is proposed, to both implement and assimilate the information within the organization, an "Information Update Automatic System" (IUAS) which utilizes Internet (INTRANET) technologies.
