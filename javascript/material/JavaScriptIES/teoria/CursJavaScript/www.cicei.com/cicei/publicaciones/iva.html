Abstract

Within the context of the '1st Program for Innovation and Educational Quality' of the University of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria (ULPGC), whose goal is "to promote electronic teaching and learning action, as well as capitalise the investment made in the ATM network at the University", the INNOVA Pilot Project was started. The aim of this project is:

  1. To facilitate the development for on-line course materials for teachers and lecturers interested in educational innovation but with little time or few resources to start learning about these procedures.
  2. To promote a global proposal for on-line learning resources at campus level in order to minimise duplicated effort, to facilitate fast downloading of course materials into the Web, to create consistent designs and to simplify technical support.
  3. To offer a default answer as a basis for discussion and enhancement, which makes it easier to move towards an educational innovation that involves all lecturing staff.
    In order to achieve this goal, and taking as a starting point the metaphor of a 'Virtual Classroom', the following stages have been defined:
    • Stage I. Design and implementation of the IVA enviroment (Interfaz Virtual de Aula,Classroom Virtual Interface). (FINISHED).
      • 1.a. User Navigating Interface (Instructional design)
      • 1.b. Code generator for Web Pages (Templates)
    • Stage II. Putting into practice the IVA enviroment by means of selecting a particular training action. (IN ACTION).
    • Stage III. Development of a pedagogical methodology that provides the 'Guidelines for developing on-line IVA courses'. (UNDER DEVELOPMENT).

As basis software for the implementation of the IVA enviroment, WebCT has been chosen; this software was developed at the University of British Columbia in Vancouver (Canada).
The 'Interuniversity Master by Distance Mode of Teaching English as a Foreign Language' has been chosen as the context for putting into practice the IVA enviroment (Stage II). The first edition of the Masters offered by the Australian Institute started in February 1998 and the degree is awared by six Spanish universities: University of the Basque Country, University of Vigo, University of Rovira I Virgili (Tarragona), University of Granada, University Antonio de Nebrija (Madrid), and University of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria. This study program is an innovative project in Spain, both in the field of postgraduate education and in the field of distance learning education.

During the first edition of the Marter of TEFL, in stage II, the students from the Canary Islands have been chosen as the validation prototype for the IVA enviroment. It is expected that during the second edition of the Master of TEFL (January 1999), its Web version will be offered all around Spain.


The IVA Enviroment

The IVA (Interfaz Virtual de Aula, Classroom Virtual Interface) is a WebCT based common interface that works as a rapid online course development tool. It provides an organised set of educational and administration tools to make it easy for the faculty to implement online courses. Anyway, they have the option to change this inteface, creating their own WebCT based courses, avoiding the IVA layer.

An IVA based online course consists of 3 main modules:

  • Public Information. Details about the specific course that the designer provides for public access, to let students know what is it about, evaluation, etc.
  • Course contents and tools
    • Virtual Classroom. This is the most important part of IVA. Here are the Course Notes, Glossary, Index, Seft-tests, Videos, Assigments, Quizzes, etc.
    • Course Tools. General Resources, Software, Search tool, etc.
    • Virtual Meeting Point. Communication Tools such as Bulleting Board, Mail, Chat, Netmeeting, Group Presentations, etc.
    • Calendar. Important dates related to the course.
    • News. Last events.
    • Site Map. To facilitate the navigation inside IVA.
    • Help. Information about IVA, WebCT, Internet, etc.
  • Feedback survey. In orden to evaluate and improve the course quality, and the quality of the IVA enviroment itself.

A main IVA Web page (http://innova.cicei.com) is also provided as a "starting point" in order to offer common resources for all the courses. It provides several facilities such as Access to the Courses, Manuals about IVA and WebCT, Support and Seminars.

IVA Applied to the Interuniversity TEFL Master

The 'Interuniversity Master by Distance Mode of Teaching English as a Foreign Language' (TEFL) is an excelent platform to test the IVA interface. There are a lot of on-line courses on the Internet related to Language Learning, specially English. It provides a good way to test the latest Multimedia technologies on the Net, including streaming audio and video, such as Real Media and videoconferencing and colaboration tools such as NetMeeting. We can even broadcast live contents on-line. It also offers us the posibility to deal with students from six different places in Spain.
